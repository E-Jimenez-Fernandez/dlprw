The CSVs in the previously distributed Monte Carlo archive are from this executed program. q05/q95 are empirical 5th/95th percentiles across repetitions, not confidence intervals. DP2 q_norm means normalized redundancy correction factors, NOT effective coefficients q/sigma on original scale. DP2 classic uses (X-min)/sample SD. Matched DP2 uses same min-max Z as DLp-RW. Stops at abs difference of successive mean scores <= 1e-8 or unchanged order; cycles and max 100 iterations flagged, last evaluated scores retained. All indicators positive. SVD relative tolerance 1e-10. Duplicate labels may tie numerically. Rankings compared by Kendall tau-b. Deletion recalculates normalization and weights on the 90% retained rows. Duplicate scenarios paired with their source scenarios. Mean weights and sd describe Monte Carlo distribution, not importance.
